# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is international recruiters and hiring managers evaluating André for remote Data Analytics and Business Intelligence roles. Secondary visitors include freelance clients who need to understand his ability to turn operational problems into reliable evidence.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio lets a visitor quickly understand André's professional positioning, inspect one complete project at a time, and continue the conversation through LinkedIn. Success means the visitor can switch cases from one clear index and judge each problem, approach, result, and insight without hidden modals.

## Positioning

André combines more than eight years of operational discipline in the Brazilian Army with hands-on Data Analytics work. The portfolio demonstrates how he reconciles incomplete data and converts it into decisions, rather than presenting a gallery of tools alone.

## Operating Context

Visitors usually arrive from a job application or professional profile, scan the portfolio on desktop or mobile, compare several projects, and then open LinkedIn, GitHub, email, or Upwork. The owner regularly adds new projects and images directly in the repository.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted on GitHub Pages; no backend or build step.
- English, Portuguese, and Spanish interface and project content; English is the default language.
- Projects must remain easy to add, remove, and reorder from one documented data file.
- Every project may contain one or many evidence images and may omit a public repository.
- LinkedIn is the primary conversion action.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name André Valenga and the existing portrait.
- Preserve the factual, direct, evidence-led voice already present in the case studies.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, employment claims, metrics, credentials, or availability details.

## Evidence on Hand

- Five complete multilingual case studies in their own folders under `content/projects/`.
- Each project's real screenshots live beside its project information file.
- Biography, statistics, skills, certifications, and contact details in `assets/js/i18n.js`.
- Public repositories exist for the Grain Cooperative and Crop Yield projects; absence of a repository for other projects must not be disguised.

## Product Principles

- Lead with evidence, not tool names.
- Keep every selected project complete and understandable in one focused view.
- Keep maintenance simple enough for the owner to perform safely.
- Treat multilingual and mobile experiences as first-class.
- Preserve factual integrity over promotional exaggeration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and keyboard operation. Respect reduced-motion preferences, expose interface state programmatically, and describe project imagery by what it demonstrates whenever a specific caption is available.
